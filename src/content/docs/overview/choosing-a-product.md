# Choosing a product

Choose a Night library when your application needs functionality that runs in your own environment and is managed as part of its codebase.

Choose a Night service when you want a hosted capability with an integration boundary instead of an embedded dependency.

If you are evaluating an unfamiliar product, begin with its overview, identify the data or runtime boundary it needs, and then follow the relevant guide. Reference documentation is best used when you need implementation-level detail.
